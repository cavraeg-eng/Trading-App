# Visual Generation Guide

Detailed prompts for generating all visual types for market research reports.

## Core Visuals (Generate First)

### 1. Market Growth Trajectory Chart

```
Bar chart showing market growth from 2020 to 2034, with historical bars in dark blue (2020-2024) and projected bars in light blue (2025-2034). Y-axis shows market size in billions USD. Include CAGR annotation for both historical and projected periods. Professional, clean design suitable for business report.
```

**Usage:** Chapter 2: Market Size & Growth Analysis

---

### 2. TAM/SAM/SOM Diagram

```
TAM SAM SOM concentric circles diagram. Outer circle TAM Total Addressable Market, middle circle SAM Serviceable Addressable Market, inner circle SOM Serviceable Obtainable Market. Each labeled with acronym and full description. Blue gradient from dark (center) to light (outer). Professional consulting-style design.
```

**Usage:** Chapter 2: Market Size & Growth Analysis

---

### 3. Porter's Five Forces

```
Porter's Five Forces diagram with center box 'Competitive Rivalry' connected to four surrounding boxes: Threat of New Entrants (top), Bargaining Power of Suppliers (left), Bargaining Power of Buyers (right), Threat of Substitutes (bottom). Color code by rating: High=red, Medium=yellow, Low=green. Professional business diagram style.
```

**Usage:** Chapter 4: Competitive Landscape

---

### 4. Competitive Positioning Matrix

```
2x2 competitive positioning matrix with X-axis 'Market Focus (Niche to Broad)' and Y-axis 'Solution Approach (Product to Platform)'. Plot 8-10 competitors as labeled circles of varying sizes representing market share. Include quadrant labels. Professional business consulting style.
```

**Usage:** Chapter 4: Competitive Landscape

---

### 5. Risk Heatmap

```
Risk heatmap matrix. X-axis Impact (Low to Critical), Y-axis Probability (Unlikely to Very Likely). Color gradient: Green (low risk) to Red (critical risk). Plot 10-12 risks as labeled points at appropriate positions. Professional risk management visualization.
```

**Usage:** Chapter 8: Risk Analysis

---

### 6. Executive Summary Infographic

```
Professional executive summary infographic for market research report. Modern data visualization style, blue and green color scheme. Clean minimalist design with icons representing market size, growth rate, key opportunities, and competitive landscape. Suitable for business consulting report.
```

**Usage:** Executive Summary

---

## Additional Visuals (Generate as Needed)

### Regional Market Breakdown

```
Pie chart or treemap showing market breakdown by region: North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, Rest of World. Include percentage labels and market size in billions. Professional business style with blue color palette.
```

---

### Segment Growth Comparison

```
Bar chart comparing growth rates across market segments. Y-axis shows CAGR percentage, X-axis shows segment names. Include both historical and projected growth bars. Professional consulting style.
```

---

### Industry Trends Timeline

```
Timeline visualization showing industry trends from 2020-2030. Horizontal timeline with trend events marked at appropriate dates. Include category color coding: Technology (blue), Regulatory (orange), Market (green). Professional business style.
```

---

### PESTLE Analysis Diagram

```
PESTLE analysis hexagonal diagram with six segments: Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, Environmental. Each segment contains key factors and trend arrows. Professional business consulting style.
```

---

### Market Share Pie Chart

```
Pie chart showing market share distribution among top 10 competitors. Include percentage labels and company names. "Others" segment for remaining share. Professional business style with varied color palette.
```

---

### Customer Segmentation Chart

```
Treemap or pie chart showing customer segmentation breakdown. Include segment names, sizes, and growth indicators. Professional business consulting style.
```

---

### Technology Roadmap

```
Technology roadmap showing evolution from current state (2024) to future state (2030). Horizontal timeline with technology milestones, adoption curves, and key innovations. Professional technology consulting style.
```

---

### Regulatory Timeline

```
Timeline showing regulatory evolution. Vertical or horizontal timeline with regulatory milestones, effective dates, and impact assessments. Include color coding for severity: High impact (red), Medium (yellow), Low (green).
```

---

### Opportunity Matrix

```
2x2 opportunity matrix with X-axis 'Ability to Win' and Y-axis 'Market Attractiveness'. Plot opportunities as labeled circles. Quadrant labels: 'Prioritize' (high-high), 'Evaluate' (low-high), 'Monitor' (high-low), 'Defer' (low-low). Professional business style.
```

---

### Implementation Timeline

```
Gantt chart or implementation timeline showing project phases over 18-24 months. Include phases, milestones, resource allocation bars, and dependencies. Professional project management style.
```

---

### Financial Projections Chart

```
Line and bar combination chart showing financial projections 2024-2030. Bars for annual revenue, line for growth rate. Include conservative, base, and optimistic scenarios in different colors. Professional financial consulting style.
```

## Visual Quality Standards

### Resolution
- Minimum: 300 DPI
- Preferred: Vector (PDF) or high-resolution PNG

### Color Palette
- Primary: Blues (#1E3A5F, #3B82F6, #93C5FD)
- Secondary: Greens (#059669, #34D399)
- Accents: Orange (#F59E0B), Red (#EF4444)
- Neutral: Grays (#1F2937, #6B7280, #F3F4F6)

### Typography
- Headings: Sans-serif (Inter, Helvetica, Arial)
- Body: Clean, readable
- Minimum font size: 10pt for labels

### Labeling Requirements
- All axes must be labeled with units
- Legends must be clear and positioned appropriately
- Data point labels where helpful
- Source attribution in figure captions

### Accessibility
- Colorblind-friendly palettes
- Sufficient contrast (4.5:1 minimum)
- Don't rely solely on color to convey information
